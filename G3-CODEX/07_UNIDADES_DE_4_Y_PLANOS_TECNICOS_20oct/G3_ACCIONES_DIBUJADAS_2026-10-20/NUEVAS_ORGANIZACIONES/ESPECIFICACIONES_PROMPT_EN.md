# De las acciones a organizaciones nuevas

Fuente de acciones: G3_TEJIDOS_A_PLANTA_40_2026-10-18/LEEME.md. Hipótesis de proyecto, no encuesta ni comportamientos observados. El catálogo anterior usaba un centro común y búsqueda angular de dormitorios; este ensayo cambia las relaciones espaciales, no solo los ángulos. No se representa como origen retrospectivo de los 40 modelos anteriores.

20 acciones agrupadas en 10 situaciones relacionadas, con dos respuestas A/B por situación: 20 plantas conceptuales nuevas. Cada vivienda admite todas las funciones domésticas; el énfasis de una acción no elimina las restantes. Llegar y salir comparten un único nodo de acceso. Los dibujos no son planos medidos ni modelos comprobados. Deben revisarse antes de reconstruirse en CAD.

## 01 · Dormir mientras otra sigue

A01 dormir · A06 entrevista · A10 hablar en privado

Relación que organiza: ruido → filtro → descanso.

**A · banda de silencio**

One long, slightly bowed dwelling in two unequal parallel bands. All FOUR private bedrooms are in a linear staggered row along the upper exterior facade, with four small curved bay seats facing actual outside. Beneath them runs a THICK occupied storage/acoustic threshold, broken only at four short screened room entrances. Shared kitchen, dining and lounge occupy a continuous inhabited lower exterior strip; quiet video interview occurs at a private wall desk, close conversation in a screened END pocket, not at a central hub. Entrance from left lower edge. This is a row/parallel-band topology, absolutely not four arms around a center.

**B · dos pares separados**

A gently folded Z-shaped connected floor, TWO pairs of private external bedrooms at opposite ends rather than four corners around center. Two shared rooms connected by an occupied acoustic elbow: an active kitchen/dining room at one end, an intimate sitting/phone room at the other; a short thick storage bend interrupts direct sound. A real lounge and kitchen both present. Bathrooms on the outside of the bend, doors off a screened short service threshold. Visible chains of spaces instead of a round central room. Interior figures show sleep versus late conversation.

## 02 · Vestirse y guardar

A02 vestirse · A05 guardar ropa

Relación que organiza: guardar → espesor habitable → intimidad.

**A · pared que se ocupa**

A narrow elongated apartment with four private bedrooms on the outer north flank arranged as an uneven row, each bedroom has a concave walk-in wardrobe recess on its inner edge, accessible from inside the bedroom. The four storage recesses form a continuous thick, scalloped buffer against a broad shared south living/kitchen strip. The middle is NOT a corridor: a long dining surface and distributed small lounge pockets inhabit it. Solid and ghost clothing bodies reveal concave changing-space movement. Wet rooms at the far west external service edge, entry at south-west, not central.

**B · dos espesores**

Two unequal gently bent parallel inhabited strips, each strip includes TWO exterior private bedrooms and its concave clothes-storage spine. Between them an off-center shared domestic street WIDENS in one end into lounge and at the other into kitchen/dining. The strips are staggered longitudinally rather than a four-wing radial arrangement. Changing niches and wardrobe doors belong to private rooms, visitors cannot walk through dressing areas. Four exterior window seats. Service bathrooms at an outer end. Show the clothing/bed/door movement traces.

## 03 · Mojar y secar

A03 ducharse · A04 secar

Relación que organiza: agua → ventilación → secado.

**A · borde húmedo**

An asymmetric shallow L-shaped apartment. Four bedrooms are staggered along the long exterior leg, two aligned in one part and two in another, all have exterior light and concave small bay seats. Bathrooms, laundry and kitchen form one coherent narrow service edge on the OUTER short leg. A screened small exterior service drying ledge directly adjoins laundry, clearly outdoor, no laundry occupying lounge. Common dining and lounge form inhabited connected spaces beside the L, no empty large courtyard and no central radial living room. Arrival clear at L end. Show a ghost path shower→laundry→outside drying.

**B · dos extremos ventilados**

An oblong gently zigzag floor with two SMALL exterior service ends: bathroom/laundry on one end, second bathroom on the other, shared kitchen positioned along lower edge. Four private bedrooms distributed in a staggered linear sequence along upper/outside edges, not radial. Lounge and dining are separate but connected widened portions of shared strip. A tiny screened drying ledge is outside the envelope next to laundry. Show wet/dry paths along the edge without crossing cooking or table work. Avoid splitting sleeping rooms from actual daylight.

## 04 · Cocinar y recoger

A07 cocinar · A19 recoger

Relación que organiza: preparar → compartir → limpiar.

**A · cocina en paralelo**

Four bedrooms in an offset exterior row above a broad long shared band. Within shared band a LONG linear kitchen counter hugs the external lower wall, dining table is PARALLEL with it with clear working passage, lounge occupies a spatially distinct end. Dishwasher/sink/waste at one end of counter: dirty returns don't cut through the cooking standing zone. Entrance at short side, two screened edge bathrooms on that end. Drawing shows simultaneous cooking and clearing ghost positions. No center with four bedroom arms.

**B · cocina en el pliegue**

One continuous double-elbow shared room in a shallow asymmetrical S, kitchen along exterior of first elbow, dining in a second side bay, communal lounge beyond at a quiet end. Four exterior private bedrooms form TWO short straight staggered rows on different external sides, with actual outside windows and curving window seats. Wet services are on an exterior end. No island bathroom, no rotational symmetry. Show food preparation→table→sink traces using distinct passing sides rather than colliding center routes.

## 05 · Comer y conversar

A08 comer · A09 conversar en grupo

Relación que organiza: mesa → estancia → elección.

**A · mesa longitudinal**

An elongated irregular apartment, four outside bedrooms staggered as a LINEAR fringe on upper facade, shared common space as a broad inhabited band below. A long communal dining/work table follows the length with clear circulation on BOTH sides; kitchen at one end, distinct lounge soft seating at the OTHER END. Table is not a pedestal at the center of radial rooms. Wide inhabited bays connected longitudinally. Bathrooms at exterior short end, entry through lower flank. Show one choosing to join meal, one passing without joining, two talking in lounge through temporal ghost overlays.

**B · dos conversaciones**

An asymmetric two-room common sequence: a kitchen/dining room near entrance and a second living/conversation room offset laterally, joined by a generous curved wide doorway. Four private rooms grouped in two pairs along upper and lower OUTSIDE edges with staggered lengths, not four detached spokes; all window seats exterior. A low storage thickening mediates sightline at join without blocking. Bathrooms on service flank. Show simultaneous small talk and dining, two actual shared attractors rather than a central hub.

## 06 · Dibujar sin desaparecer

A11 dibujar · A06 entrevista

Relación que organiza: luz → tablero → compartir o cerrar.

**A · franja de dibujo**

A linear four-person dwelling where four PRIVATE bedrooms all occupy the exterior upper flank, each has private hinged short-edge A1 drawing desk and curved small window seat. A second shared DAYLIGHT work strip occupies a LONG exterior lower facade alongside lounge and a kitchen/dining end. The shared daylight strip is wide enough for occupied tables AND a continuous clear passage and is not a dark corridor. The spatial choice is private drawing vs shared drawing along a facade, not four rooms around center. Wet services at an exterior end. Trace unfolded paper and drawing activity on actual desks.

**B · dos claros de trabajo**

A broad gently zigzagging apartment with TWO widened shared working-living pockets at two different positions along a clear connecting domestic route, kitchen/dining off one pocket, quieter shared drawing off the other. Four private rooms attached in a staggered longitudinal chain along external facades, not radiating from one point. Each exterior bedroom has rectangular bed, concave wardrobe and small curved window seat, private hinged drawing desk. Show a remote interview behind a closed bedroom door while common paper/model work occurs elsewhere; distinguish sound protection from isolation.

## 07 · Hacer maqueta y guardarla

A12 guardar maqueta · A13 cortar

Asymmetric four-person L plan with a broad common WORKING edge along the external lower long facade. Shared cutting/model table directly beside a deep model-storage wall, so unfinished model can be slid on trays into storage without crossing circulation. Kitchen/dining at an adjacent END, real lounge at another end. Four external private bedrooms are arranged in two unequal staggered rows along upper exterior and short outside leg; not a radial clover. Two bathrooms screened on exterior service flank. Trace model making, cutting mats and sliding storage footprints, no foamboard blocking doorway.

**B · alcobas de trabajo**

Four external bedroom cells in an irregular but aligned sequence along one long edge of a folded strip. Opposite edge has TWO broad shared working alcoves separated by deep model-storage cupboards; alcoves open directly to a continuous lounge/dining space, kitchen at end. No separate dark workshop box or corridor. Desk/table deployment ghost footprints remain out of clear through route. Outside windows at each private room, two bathroom service rooms at extreme exterior edge. Spatial difference is distributed storage/work alcoves instead of one large workshop.

## 08 · Coser, reparar la tela

A14 coser · A20 guardar herramientas

Relación que organiza: extender → tensar → recoger.

**A · mesa y pared textil**

A shallow S-shaped apartment. Four private bedrooms are in TWO parallel staggered outside pairs, not four radial arms. A long shared fabric-making table runs alongside an exterior communal window, with shallow fabric/tool storage on adjacent wall; textiles are shown flat on table with repeated fold positions. Kitchen/dining and lounge are two other continuous widenings of the S, not one central nucleus. Two screened exterior bathrooms near entry end. Small outside fabric shading at actual facade windows, not a tent replacing bedroom walls. Show hands moving and fabrics gathered away from entry.

**B · trabajar en el umbral**

A four-person dwelling organized as two two-bedroom longitudinal clusters linked by a WIDE INHABITED common porch-like workroom, enclosed as living space, with long fabric repair worktop along exterior edge and storage beneath. The connection is a furnished room with clear side passage, not a bridge corridor or central circle. A kitchen/dining flank extends from one cluster, lounge lies next to the workroom; four private bedrooms retain actual outside light and curved small seats. Two bathrooms are on outer service edges, never in middle of workroom. Show sewing, folding and packing as temporal overlays.

## 09 · Reparar sin ocupar el paso

A15 reparar bicicleta · A20 guardar herramientas

Relación que organiza: entrada → apoyo → herramientas.

**A · ensanche de llegada**

A four-bedroom elongated dwelling, bedrooms in a staggered exterior row, long shared living/dining strip with kitchen at far side. Entrance near one END widens into an EDGE work niche with ONE bicycle propped along wall and a tool cabinet, all outside clear arrival route. Draw the bicycle from above in that niche, not center of lounge. The working niche is visually open to shared living but not part of bedroom access. Bathroom/service band on another exterior edge, discreet doors. Show arrival and repair path do not overlap, dotted deployment boundaries. Keep residential scale, not a bike warehouse.

**B · taller que comparte el borde**

An asymmetrical compact comb plan: four private bedrooms form a NON-radial staggered fringe along the upper exterior edge, shared lounge/dining and kitchen along lower facade. At one outside END a small workshop/service bay can be approached directly from the SAME entry threshold and also from common room; bicycle and tools sit in lateral bay, no passage runs through repair footprint. Two bathroom rooms are on an outer service side well away from social views. Show alternative occupation: repair with wheel removed on wall hooks vs tools packed and niche available for receiving bags. These are plan ghosts, not extra bicycles.

## 10 · Llegar, recibir y salir

A16 llegar · A17 recibir · A18 salir

Relación que organiza: un acceso → dos grados de intimidad.

**A · llegada con bifurcación**

An irregular four-person apartment with ONE clear external entrance on lower short side. Immediately inside one occupied vestibule with a side bag-storage niche branches into a public route to shared kitchen/dining/lounge and a more protected short route to four external private bedrooms arranged in a staggered linear band. Arrival and departure are the SAME NODE, do not draw a separate exit as a new attractor. A visitor can sit in lounge without passing bedroom doors. Service bathrooms on outer edge entered off screened route. Footprint asymmetrical linear elbow, not a radial center. Draw guest waiting, resident putting bag away, and someone leaving as pale temporal alternatives.

**B · recibir en una estancia**

A long shallow bent apartment with entrance opening to a genuinely furnished common room at ONE END, shared dining and kitchen extend along an external side, FOUR private bedrooms arranged in two aligned staggered groups farther along the length. A storage/curved wall creates a visual turn before private thresholds. The route from entrance to kitchen and lounge is public, bedrooms are behind that turn, no long empty corridor. Arrival and departure share the SAME entry. Bathroom edge tucked near service flank, not facing living. Visitors can stop without occupying the through route. Show group receiving food delivery and a resident quietly passing to a private room without forced interaction.
